\section{Main reproducibility files}\label{main-reproducibility-files}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \texttt{code/python/run\_meta\_controller.py}: compact replication of the Phase 10
  expert aggregation and delayed-loss evaluation from processed expert
  forecasts.
\item
  \texttt{code/R/make\_figures.R}: complete ggplot2 figure pipeline for all six
  manuscript figures.
\item
  \texttt{phase10\_monthly\_regret\_*.csv}: month-level regret inputs used for
  cumulative-regret figures.
\item
  \texttt{phase10\_model\_metrics.csv}: pooled model evaluation metrics.
\item
  \texttt{phase10\_simulation\_summary.csv}: delayed-feedback simulation results.
\end{itemize}
