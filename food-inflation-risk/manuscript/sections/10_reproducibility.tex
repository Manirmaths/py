\section{Main reproducibility files}\label{main-reproducibility-files}

\begin{itemize}
\item
  code/python/run_meta_controller.py: compact replication of the Phase 10
  expert aggregation and delayed-loss evaluation from processed expert
  forecasts.
\item
  code/R/make\_figures.R: complete ggplot2 figure pipeline for all six
  manuscript figures.
\item
  phase10\_monthly\_metrics files: month-level losses, coverage, and regret
  inputs used for cumulative-regret figures.
\item
  phase10\_model\_metrics.csv and phase10\_period\_metrics.csv: pooled
  and period-specific evaluation metrics.
\item
  phase10\_simulation\_summary.csv: delayed-feedback simulation results.
\end{itemize}
