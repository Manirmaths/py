Funding: To be completed before submission.

Competing interests: The author declares no competing interests, subject

Ethics approval: Not applicable. The study uses publicly available
aggregate price data.

Data availability: The NBS Selected Food Price Watch and associated fuel
and transport series are publicly available from the National Bureau of
Statistics microdata catalogue. WFP market-price observations and the
World Bank Real-Time Food Prices catalogue are publicly accessible.
Processed analysis files and code are included in the accompanying
replication package; a permanent public repository and DOI should be
created before submission.

Author contributions: Maniru Ibrahim conceived the study, constructed
the datasets, developed the forecasting design, performed the analysis,
interpreted the results, and drafted the manuscript. This statement
should be revised if co-authors are added.
